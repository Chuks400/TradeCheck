# Research context: Shenzhou International (HKEX 02313)

These **public, company-reported figures** establish the scale of Shenzhou's international operations. They **do not** prove that its current trade document review is manual or defective.

| Verified public figure | Reported value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Export/international sales, first half 2026 | RMB 10.722 billion, 75.6% of all sales | Shenzhou 2026 Interim Report, printed p. 9 |
| Transport and customs clearance expense, H1 2026 | RMB 224.012 million | Shenzhou 2026 Interim Report, financial statements, printed p. 26 |
| Transport and customs clearance expense, H1 2025 | RMB 195.260 million | Same source, comparable H1 2025 column |
| Five largest customers' share of sales, H1 2026 | about 87% | Shenzhou 2026 Interim Report, printed p. 42 |
| Main suppliers, 2025 | 171 (Vietnam 48.0%, China 47.3%, other regions 4.7%) | 2025 ESG Report, printed p. 64 |
| Staff costs as percentage of sales, H1 2026 | 32.0% (H1 2025: 29.4%) | Shenzhou 2026 Interim Report, printed p. 11 |

**Derived calculation:** Transportation and customs clearance expenses increased by approximately **14.7%** between H1 2025 and H1 2026 (224.012 / 195.260 − 1). These expenses combine transport and customs and cannot be attributed specifically to document errors.

## What still needs evidence

- Samples or templates of real-world export documents and their legal requirements (official templates may be used).
- Baseline human review time, observed in an experiment.
- Test errors introduced deliberately into synthetic documents, labelled by the team.
- A measured comparison of manual checking and the proposed prototype.
- Evidence of Shenzhou's actual workflow (do not assume a missing AI system simply because public reports do not mention one).

## Ethical handling

All example files in this project say `SYNTHETIC SAMPLE – NOT SHENZHOU INTERNATIONAL DATA`. Never describe them as company internal records. Final report should separate public facts, industry estimates, simulated values, and experimentally measured results.
